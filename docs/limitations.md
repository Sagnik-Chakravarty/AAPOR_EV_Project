# Limitations and Measurement Cautions

This project evaluates the relationship between LLM-generated sentiment and observed digital trace sentiment. It should be interpreted as a measurement-validity project rather than a definitive estimate of public opinion about electric vehicles.

## 1. Digital Trace Data Are Not Representative Surveys

Reddit and news data do not represent the general population. Platform users differ from the broader public, and online participation is shaped by interest, identity, attention, and controversy.

## 2. Platform Context Matters

Different Reddit communities may have systematically different attitudes toward electric vehicles. For example, EV-focused communities, Tesla-focused communities, and car-skeptical communities may not be directly comparable without accounting for community context.

## 3. LLM Outputs Are Not Observed Public Opinion

LLM-generated sentiment estimates may reflect model training data, prompt framing, or generalized stereotypes about electric vehicles. They should not be treated as direct substitutes for survey responses or observed discourse.

## 4. Sentiment Is a Simplified Measure

Sentiment scores reduce complex public discourse to positive, neutral, or negative signals. This can miss policy concerns, infrastructure issues, environmental attitudes, brand-specific sentiment, and economic considerations.

## 5. Source Coverage Is Uneven

News and social media sources vary in volume, topic emphasis, and audience. Differences across sources may reflect platform selection as much as underlying public sentiment.

## 6. Interpretation

The project is best interpreted as a comparison of measurement signals. Its main contribution is showing where LLM-generated estimates and observed discourse align or diverge, not producing a population-representative estimate of EV attitudes.
